Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every lesson, every practice and every vocab deck is free.

Do I need an account?

Not to start. You can read and practise any lesson without one. A free account saves the lessons you finish and gives you the vocab flashcards and a daily vocab review.

Is this Central Catalan?

Yes. The lessons teach standard Central Catalan, the variety spoken in Barcelona.

How long does A1 take?

It depends on your pace, and there is no deadline. The course is {{ lesson_count }} lessons, and you can take them in any order.

What comes after A1?

A2 is not here yet. Once you finish A1, keep reviewing your vocab every day so the words stay fresh.
